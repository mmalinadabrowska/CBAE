Who are we?

A studio of four designers, curators and researchers working across culture, learning and futures.

Helen Job

Helen is an interdisciplinary researcher, strategist, and creative leader working at the intersection of cultural change, innovation, and future possibilities. Her work helps organisations, institutions, and creative communities understand shifting cultural landscapes, uncover new opportunities, and build more thoughtful, future-ready strategies. She’s particularly passionate about shaping new knowledge systems, with a growing focus on alternative design education and more cooperative futures. Helen recently co-founded Parallel Pedagogies, a research and design lab for the future of learning.

Shanu Walpita

Shanu is a curator, educator, and foresight director exploring the glitched and looped intersections of technology, culture, and identity. A former Course Leader at UAL and co-curator of the State of Fashion Biennale, she is currently the editorial director of Softer Digital Futures Protocols and co-founder of Parallel Pedagogies, a research lab exploring the future of learning. Shanu speaks, writes, and advises globally for international festivals, publications, and advisory boards.

Anne Zhou

Anne is a maker, curator, and educator working across spatial design, exhibition-making and objects. Her practice moves between large-scale cultural projects and independent research-led work. She has worked with institutions including the V&A and the Shanghai Biennale, and most recently co-curated the State of Fashion Biennale 2026 in the Netherlands.

Malina Dabrowska

Malina is a design leader and futures strategist with over a decade of experience turning long-horizon research into action. She works across strategy, spatial design, film, exhibitions, and tools, helping organisations imagine and build resilient, regenerative futures across climate, technology, and the built environment.
