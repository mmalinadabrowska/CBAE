Placeholder manifesto. Set in Let Her Go, waiting for the real text.

One. A meeting is not a delivery mechanism. If the only thing that happens in the room is the transfer of information, we have wasted a room.

Two. We are against the summary that replaces the argument, the recap that flattens the disagreement, the bullet point that survives the conversation it came from.

Three. We keep the friction. Paper jams, toner streaks, people talking over each other, someone changing their mind halfway through a sentence.

Four. Everything worth saying takes longer than it should, and is worth the time it takes.

Five. When it could have been an email, we will send an email. This is not that.
